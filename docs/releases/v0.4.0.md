## New

- **Scan schedule (cron):** `SCAN_SCHEDULE` takes a cron expression instead of a fixed interval, e.g.
  `0 3 * * *` for every night at 3:00, `0 3 * * sun` for Sundays, or `@daily`. The time zone comes from `TZ`
  (e.g. `Europe/Berlin`). With a schedule there is no scan at container start, unless there is no result yet –
  so an update doesn't wake up your disks in the middle of the day. *Scan now* works as always.
- The page shows when the next scan is due, and the container log says at start when Dustbunny scans.
